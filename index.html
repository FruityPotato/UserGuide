Meeting Room Quick Start

Connecting to a Yealink screen.

Four ways to bring a TASAMA meeting room into your Teams call — pick the one that fits how you're working today.

Choose a method

Book the room from your Outlook invitation

★ Recommended ⏱ Pre-scheduled

The cleanest path. Add the meeting room as a location in Outlook — at meeting time, the room appears on the screen and you join with a single tap. No codes, no scanning.

1
In Outlook, open or create a meeting and click Location.
2
Pick a meeting room from the suggestions. Outlook only shows rooms that are free during your selected time.
Need a specific room? Open Scheduling Assistant to see live availability across all TASAMA rooms before deciding.
3
Send the invitation. The room is now booked and the meeting will appear on its schedule.
4
At meeting time, walk into the room and tap Join on the Yealink screen.
Sharing your screen
Use the Yealink Connector (fastest) Plug the USB-C dongle into your laptop, wait for the indicator to glow, then press the touch button to start sharing instantly.
Share through Teams Join the meeting on your laptop alongside the room (set audio to "Don't use audio" to avoid echo) and use the Share button in Teams.

Join with Meeting ID & Passcode

★ External meetings ⏱ ~30 seconds

If the room wasn't added to the invitation — or if it's an external Teams meeting — you can still bring the room in using the Meeting ID and Passcode from the invitation email.

1
On the Yealink screen, tap Join with an ID (the # icon in the side menu).
2
Open the Teams meeting invitation in your email. Scroll to the bottom — you'll find the Meeting ID and Passcode under "Microsoft Teams meeting".
3
Type both values into the screen.
4
Tap Join Teams meeting. The room is now in the call.
Quick tip The Meeting ID is a 12-digit number (e.g. 329 761 338 828) and the Passcode is a short alphanumeric string. Spaces in the ID are optional — type it however you like.

Scan the QR code on the screen

★ Walk-up & go ⏱ ~10 seconds

Every Yealink screen displays a unique QR code in the top-left corner. Scanning it from the Teams app on your phone or laptop adds the room as a participant to your current meeting.

1
Start or join a Teams meeting on your phone or laptop.
2
Point your camera at the QR code in the top-left corner of the Yealink screen, labelled "Join your meeting with this room".
3
Confirm in Teams — the room joins your meeting automatically.
4
Invite other participants from your device as needed. Use the room for audio, video, and the big screen.
Why use this method? It's the fastest path when you walk into a free room and decide to start a meeting on the spot — no need to type IDs or open Outlook.

Join from your laptop, route audio through the room

★ Drive from your laptop ⏱ ~20 seconds

Teams detects nearby Yealink rooms via Bluetooth proximity. This lets you control the meeting from your laptop while the room provides audio, camera, and the big screen — best when you want both your familiar laptop view and full room hardware.

1
Open the Teams meeting on your laptop — but don't click Join yet.
2
On the pre-join screen, look at the audio options on the right and select Room audio.
Teams will show "We've detected a room device" when proximity works.
3
Choose your room from the Nearby list (e.g. Al-Raidah Tower Main 2F-MR05). If yours doesn't appear, search for it.
4
Click Join now. The Yealink screen joins automatically and your laptop becomes the controller.
⚠ Avoid the echo Room Audio routes sound through the room speakers. Don't switch back to Computer Audio while you're inside the room — your laptop mic will pick up the room speakers and create feedback.

Good to know

📅
Check before you walk in. The right side of the Yealink screen shows the room's schedule for today and tomorrow — green means available.
🔌
The Yealink Connector is universal. Pair it once and it works on every TASAMA Yealink screen.
🚪
Always end the meeting properly. Tap Leave on the screen when you're done so the room is freed up for the next user.
🎤
Mute your laptop mic in the room. If you join from both the room and your laptop, set your laptop to "Don't use audio" to avoid echo.
🆘
Connector lost or dead? Methods 2, 3, and 4 don't need it — you can still get the room into your call.
